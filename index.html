Laboratório Impossível (PT-BR) and Impossible Lab (EN) are science short-video channels. We create original 3D animations of hypothetical "What if...?" experiments with exotic elements of the periodic table, showing what science measures and predicts.

Find us on TikTok (@laboratorioimpossivel) and YouTube (@laboratorioimpossivel).

Our publishing tool

"Laboratorio Impossivel" is also the name of our internal desktop tool that uploads the videos we produce to our own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It is used only by our team and is not offered to other users.
