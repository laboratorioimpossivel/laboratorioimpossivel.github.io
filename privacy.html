Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains how the Laboratorio Impossivel publishing tool ("the App") handles data.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by our own team to publish our own videos. It does not collect data from the public or from viewers.

2. Data we access

When our account authorizes the App through TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, used only to confirm which of our accounts is connected. The App uses the video.upload and video.publish scopes only to post videos we created.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on our own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use and can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions), after which we delete the local copy.

5. Website

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

6. Contact

Message us on TikTok @laboratorioimpossivel.
